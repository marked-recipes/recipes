## Ingredients

### For the Roasted Brussels Sprouts & Grapes:

- [ ] 1 1/2 lbs Brussels sprouts, trimmed and halved (680 g)
- [ ] 2 cups seedless red grapes (300 g)
- [ ] 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- [ ] 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- [ ] 1/4 tsp freshly cracked black pepper
- [ ] 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- [ ] 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes

### For the Hot Honey Balsamic:

- [ ] 3 tbsp pure honey
- [ ] 2 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- [ ] 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- [ ] 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
- [ ] 1 tsp fresh lemon juice
- [ ] Pinch of kosher salt

### For the Burrata & Pistachio Topping:

- [ ] 8 oz burrata cheese (225 g)
- [ ] 1/3 cup shelled pistachios, roughly chopped (45 g)
- [ ] 2 tbsp fresh thyme leaves
- [ ] 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- [ ] 1/4 tsp freshly cracked black pepper

### For Garnish (optional):

- [ ] Fresh thyme leaves
- [ ] Extra chopped pistachios
- [ ] Flaky sea salt
- [ ] Freshly cracked black pepper

## Directions

- [ ] Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper.
- [ ] Toss the Brussels sprouts and grapes with olive oil, salt, black pepper, garlic powder, and red pepper flakes.
- [ ] Arrange everything cut-side down in a single layer. Roast for 25–30 minutes, turning the Brussels sprouts halfway through, until deeply caramelized and the grapes are softened and blistered.
- [ ] Meanwhile, whisk together the honey, balsamic vinegar, Dijon mustard, red pepper flakes, lemon juice, and salt. Warm gently in a small saucepan for 1–2 minutes until glossy and slightly thickened.
- [ ] Transfer the roasted Brussels sprouts and grapes to a serving platter.
- [ ] Tear the burrata into pieces and nestle it among the warm vegetables.
- [ ] Scatter the chopped pistachios and fresh thyme over the top. Drizzle with olive oil and season with freshly cracked black pepper.
- [ ] Drizzle generously with the hot honey balsamic while the burrata is still cool and creamy.
- [ ] Finish with fresh thyme, extra pistachios, and flaky sea salt, if using.
- [ ] Serve immediately while the Brussels sprouts are warm and the burrata is soft.
