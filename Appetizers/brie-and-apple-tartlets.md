## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 sheet puff pastry, thawed
- [ ] 2 cups apples (Gala, Honeycrisp, or Granny Smith), diced small
- [ ] 4-6 oz Brie cheese, cut into 12 small cubes
- [ ] 1/3 cup walnuts or pecans, chopped small (optional)
- [ ] 2 tbsp brown sugar
- [ ] 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon
- [ ] 1 large egg, beaten with 1 tbsp water (for egg wash)
- [ ] 2 tbsp honey, for drizzling
- [ ] 1 tbsp fresh thyme leaves, for garnish

## Instructions

- [ ] Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C) and lightly grease a 12-cup mini-muffin pan.
- [ ] In a small bowl, toss the diced apples, chopped walnuts, brown sugar, and ground cinnamon together until evenly coated.
- [ ] Lightly flour a work surface, unfold the puff pastry sheet, and roll gently to smooth creases. Cut into 12 equal squares (approx. 3x3 inches).
- [ ] Press each pastry square into the cups of the mini-muffin pan, leaving pastry corners pointing upwards.
- [ ] Place one cube of Brie cheese into the base of each pastry cup.
- [ ] Spoon the spiced apple mixture generously over the cheese.
- [ ] Brush exposed pastry corners lightly with the beaten egg wash.
- [ ] Bake for 15 to 20 minutes until puff pastry edges are golden brown and crisp and cheese is bubbly.
- [ ] Cool in the pan for 5 minutes, transfer to a platter, and drizzle lightly with honey and fresh thyme before serving.

## Notes

* To prevent sogginess, ensure diced apples are drained of excess liquid before placing them in pastry cups.
* Rind can be left on or removed from the Brie based on personal preference.
