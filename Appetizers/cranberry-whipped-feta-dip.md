## Ingredients
### For the Whipped Feta:
- [ ] 8 oz feta cheese, crumbled (225 g)
- [ ] 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt (120 g)
- [ ] 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- [ ] 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- [ ] 1 tsp lemon zest
- [ ] 1 small garlic clove, grated
- [ ] 1/4 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- [ ] 1 tbsp fresh thyme leaves
### For the Cranberry Topping:
- [ ] 1 cup fresh or frozen cranberries (100 g)
- [ ] 2 tbsp pure maple syrup
- [ ] 1 tbsp orange juice
- [ ] 1 tsp orange zest
- [ ] 2 tbsp water
- [ ] 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon
- [ ] Pinch of sea salt
### For the Hot Honey Drizzle:
- [ ] 1/4 cup honey (85 g)
- [ ] 1/2 tsp crushed red pepper flakes
- [ ] 1 tsp fresh lemon juice
- [ ] Pinch of sea salt

## Instructions
- [ ] Add the reta, Greek yogurt, olive oil, lemon juice, lemon zest, garlic, and black pepper to a food processor. Blend for 1-2 minutes until smooth and creamy. Fold in the fresh thyme.
- [ ] In a small saucepan, combine the cranberries, maple syrup, orange juice, orange zest, water, cinnamon, and salt.
- [ ] Simmer over medium-low heat for 6-8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the cranberries soften and begin to burst.
- [ ] Lightly mash a few berries, then let the mixture cool slightly.
- [ ] Spread the whipped feta onto a shallow serving plate or bowl, creating soft swirls with the back of a spoon.
- [ ] Spoon the warm cranberry mixture over the center of the whipped feta, allowing some of the berries and juices to spread across the surface.
- [ ] Gently warm the honey, red pepper flakes, lemon juice, and salt in a small saucepan over low heat for 1-2 minutes.
- [ ] Drizzle the hot honey generously over the feta and cranberry topping.
- [ ] Finish with toasted walnuts, fresh thyme, orange zest, flaky sea salt, and pomegranate arils if desired.
- [ ] Serve with toasted baguette slices, pita wedges, crackers, or crisp apple slices.
