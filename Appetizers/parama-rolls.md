## Ingredients
- [ ] Parma ham (prosciutto)
- [ ] Ricotta
- [ ] Green Pesto
- [ ] Cherry Tomatoes
- [ ] Arugula
- [ ] Red Onion
- [ ] Balsamic Glaze

## Instructions
- [ ] Lay out Parma ham overlapping about halfway
- [ ] Spread ricotta and pesto over each slice of Parma ham
- [ ] Add tomatoes, arugula and finely chopped red onion, then roll them up
- [ ] Slice then finish with a drizzle of balsamic glaze
