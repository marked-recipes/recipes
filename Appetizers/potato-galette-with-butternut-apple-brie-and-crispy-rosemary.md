## Ingredients
### For the Galette
- [ ] 1 sheet frozen puff pastry, thawed (about 250 g)
- [ ] 2 medium Yukon Gold potatoes, thinly sliced
- [ ] 1½ cups butternut squash, peeled and thinly sliced
- [ ] 1 medium Honeycrisp apple, thinly sliced
- [ ] 5 oz (140 g) Brie cheese, sliced
- [ ] 2 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] [ ] 1 tbsp melted butter
- [ ] 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves
- [ ] ½ tsp garlic powder
- [ ] ½ tsp smoked paprika
- [ ] Salt and black pepper, to taste
- [ ] 1 egg, beaten (for pastry edges)
### For the Crispy Rosemary
- [ ] 2 tbsp fresh rosemary leaves
- [ ] 1 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] Pinch of flaky sea salt
### For Finishing
- [ ] 1 tbsp honey
- [ ] 2 tbsp chopped toasted pecans (optional)
- [ ] 1 tsp balsamic glaze (optional)

## Instructions
- [ ] Preheat the oven: Heat to 400°F (200°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
- [ ] Prepare the vegetables: Thinly slice the potatoes and butternut squash, aiming for slices about ⅛ inch (3 mm) thick. Toss with olive oil, melted butter, garlic powder, smoked paprika, thyme, salt, and pepper.
- [ ] Prepare the apple: Thinly slice the apple and set aside. Keep the slices fairly thin so they soften while baking.
- [ ] Shape the galette: Place the puff pastry on the prepared baking sheet. Gently roll it into a slightly larger rectangle or circle, leaving the edges intact.
- [ ] Layer the filling: Arrange the potato slices over the pastry, leaving a 1½-inch (4 cm) border. Add the butternut squash and apple slices in overlapping layers, then distribute the Brie throughout.
- [ ] Fold the edges: Fold the pastry border inward over the filling, pleating as needed for a rustic look. Brush the pastry edges with beaten egg.
- [ ] Bake until golden: Bake for 35–45 minutes, until the pastry is puffed and golden and the potatoes are tender. If the pastry browns too quickly, loosely cover the edges with foil.
- [ ] Crisp the rosemary: While the galette bakes, heat 1 tablespoon olive oil in a small skillet over medium-low heat. Add the rosemary leaves and fry for 30–60 seconds until crisp. Transfer to a paper towel and sprinkle lightly with sea salt.
- [ ] Add the finishing touches: Drizzle the warm galette with honey and optional balsamic glaze. Sprinkle with crispy rosemary and toasted pecans.
- [ ] Rest and serve: Let the galette rest for 5–10 minutes before slicing. Serve warm while the Brie is creamy and the pastry is crisp.
