## Ingredients
### For the Chicken
- [ ] 1 1/2 lb (680 g) boneless, skinless chicken breasts or thighs
- [ ] 1 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] 1/2 tsp paprika
- [ ] 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- [ ] 1/2 tsp Italian seasoning
- [ ] 1/2 tsp salt
- [ ] 1/4 tsp black pepper
### For the Mushroom Sauce
- [ ] 2 tbsp unsalted butter
- [ ] 8 oz (225 g) cremini or button mushrooms, sliced
- [ ] 3 garlic cloves, minced
- [ ] 1 small shallot, finely diced
- [ ] 1/2 cup (120 ml) chicken broth
- [ ] 3/4 cup (180 ml) heavy cream
- [ ] 1 1/2 tbsp Dijon mustard
- [ ] 1/2 cup (50 g) freshly grated Asiago cheese
- [ ] 1/2 tsp fresh thyme leaves
- [ ] 1/4 tsp black pepper
- [ ] Salt, to taste
### For Garnish
- [ ] 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
- [ ] 1 tbsp freshly grated Asiago
- [ ] Fresh thyme leaves
- [ ] Freshly cracked black pepper

## Directions
- [ ] Season the chicken: Pat the chicken dry and season both sides with paprika, garlic powder, Italian seasoning, salt, and black pepper.
- [ ] Sear the chicken: Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and sear for 4–5 minutes per side, until deeply golden. Transfer to a plate; the chicken does not need to be fully cooked yet.
- [ ] Brown the mushrooms: Reduce the heat to medium and add the butter. Add the mushrooms and cook for 5–7 minutes until golden and their moisture has cooked away.
- [ ] Add aromatics: Stir in the shallot and garlic. Cook for 1–2 minutes until softened and fragrant.
- [ ] Deglaze: Pour in the chicken broth, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the skillet. Simmer for 2 minutes.
- [ ] Make the sauce: Reduce the heat to medium-low. Stir in the heavy cream and Dijon mustard. Simmer gently for 3–4 minutes.
- [ ] Add the Asiago: Gradually stir in the grated Asiago until melted and the sauce becomes creamy. Add thyme and black pepper. Taste and adjust the seasoning.
- [ ] Finish the chicken: Return the chicken and any accumulated juices to the skillet. Spoon the sauce and mushrooms over the chicken. Cover loosely and simmer for 5–7 minutes, or until the chicken reaches an internal temperature of 165°F (74°C).
- [ ] Garnish and serve: Sprinkle with fresh parsley, additional Asiago, thyme, and cracked black pepper. Serve immediately.
### Serving Suggestions
- [ ] Spoon the creamy chicken and mushrooms over mashed potatoes, buttered noodles, rice, or creamy polenta. For a lower-carb option, serve with cauliflower mash, roasted broccoli, asparagus, or zucchini noodles.

## Notes
* Slice very thick chicken breasts in half horizontally for faster, more even cooking.
* Give the mushrooms enough space in the pan so they brown instead of steaming.
* Use freshly grated Asiago for the smoothest sauce.
* Keep the sauce at a gentle simmer after adding the cheese to prevent it from becoming grainy.
* For extra depth, add 1/4 cup dry white wine after sautéing the mushrooms and let it reduce before adding the broth.
* Add a squeeze of fresh lemon juice just before serving to balance the richness.
