## Ingredients

- [ ] 2 eggs beaten
- [ ] ½ stick butter
- [ ] 1 cup sugar
- [ ] ⅔ cup uncooked oatmeal
- [ ] ¼ teaspoon salt
- [ ] 1 teaspoon vanilla
- [ ] 3 tablespoons cocoa
- [ ] 1 8’ pie shell

## Instructions

- [ ] Prepare all ingredCombine first seven ingredients as listed.
- [ ] Blend well and pour into unbaked pie shell.
- [ ] Bake at 300 for 45 minutes.
