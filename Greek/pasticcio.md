## Ingredients

- [ ] 2 pounds ground beef (80/20)
- [ ] 2 cups finely diced yellow onion
- [ ] 3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- [ ] 2 pounds ground beef (80/20)
- [ ] 1/2 teaspoon ground Aleppo pepper
- [ ] 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- [ ] 4 sprigs fresh thyme
- [ ] 1 cup dry white wine
- [ ] 3 cups crushed tomatoes
- [ ] 1 cup water
- [ ] 8 tablespoons unsalted butter
- [ ] 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- [ ] 1/2 teaspoon freshly grated nutmeg
- [ ] 1 cup whole milk
- [ ] 1 cup finely grated kefalotyri cheese
- [ ] 1 pound macaroni #1
- [ ] 4 tablespoons unsalted butter
- [ ] 9-by-13-inch casserole dish
- [ ] Olive oil (for the casserole dish)
- [ ] Black pepper

## Instructions

### Make the meat sauce
- [ ] Warm the olive oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat.
- [ ] Add the ground beef and sear without disturbing until brown, breaking it up into smaller pieces with a wooden spoon.
- [ ] Season with salt and pepper and cook until deeply browned, about 8 to 10 minutes.
- [ ] Stir in the onion and cook until softened, about 3 minutes.
- [ ] Add the Aleppo pepper and cinnamon and stir to combine.
- [ ] Tie the thyme sprigs together with butcher’s twine.
- [ ] Deglaze with kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper the wine, reduce by half, then stir in the crushed tomatoes and 1 cup water.
- [ ] Reduce the sauce to a simmer and cook until the tomatoes have begun to break down, about 30 minutes.
### Make the béchamel sauce
- [ ] In a large saucepan over medium heat, add the butter and allow to melt.
- [ ] Whisk in the flour and allow to toast for 1 minute.
- [ ] Add the nutmeg and about a quarter of the milk and whisk as the mixture cooks and bubbles.
- [ ] Add another quarter of the milk and whisk.
- [ ] Once the milk is incorporated, add the rest of the milk, kosher salt, and freshly ground black pepper, whisk again to ensure a smooth consistency.
- [ ] Remove from the heat and whisk in the kefalotyri cheese until melted.
- [ ] Whisk kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper in the eggs and set aside.
### Make the pasta
- [ ] Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and add the 1 cup grated kefalotyri cheese pasta.
- [ ] Cook the pasta for 4 minutes less than the package instructions.
### Assemble and bake
- [ ] Brush a 9-by-13-inch casserole dish with olive oil and set aside.
- [ ] Using tongs, remove the pasta from the pot and line the pasta evenly in the bottom of the prepared casserole dish, ensuring all noodles face the same direction.
- [ ] Add the melted butter and kefalotyri cheese and season with black pepper.
- [ ] Remove the bundle of thyme from the meat sauce and pour the meat sauce on top of the pasta.
- [ ] Using a rubber spatula, spread the meat sauce into an even layer without pressing down.
- [ ] Pour the béchamel over the meat sauce.
- [ ] Place the casserole dish onto the offset heat part of the grill, close the grill, and bake until the top is golden brown and puffy, 40 to 45 minutes.
- [ ] Remove from the grill and allow to cool for at least 30 minutes before cutting into slices and serving.

## Notes
* If your local market does not carry kefalotyri cheese, Pecorino Romano makes an excellent substitute!
* Oven method: Bake uncovered at 400 degrees F for 40 to 45 minutes.
