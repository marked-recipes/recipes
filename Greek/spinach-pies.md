## Ingredients

## Filling
- [ ] 2 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] 1 medium onion, finely diced
- [ ] 3 garlic cloves, minced
- [ ] 10 oz fresh spinach, chopped
- [ ] 1 cup feta cheese, crumbled
- [ ] 1 cup ricotta cheese (or cottage cheese)
- [ ] 2 large eggs, beaten
- [ ] ¼ cup fresh dill or parsley, chopped
- [ ] Salt, black pepper, and a pinch of nutmeg

## Crust & Topping:
- [ ] 1 sheet puff pastry (thawed) or 8 sheets phyllo dough
- [ ] 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese (for the golden melted top)
- [ ] 1 egg beaten with 1 tbsp milk (egg wash)

## Instructions

- [ ] **Cook Spinach:** Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Sauté the onion and garlic until soft (3–4 minutes). Add spinach in batches, cooking until completely wilted. Remove from heat, let cool slightly, and squeeze out all excess moisture using a clean towel.
- [ ] **Mix Filling:** In a bowl, combine the drained spinach mixture, feta, ricotta, eggs, herbs, pepper, and a pinch of nutmeg (go light on extra salt since feta is salty).
- [ ] **Assemble:** Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Line a 9x9-inch baking dish with parchment paper and lay down the pastry sheet, pressing slightly up the sides. Spread the spinach-feta mixture evenly over the base.
- [ ] **Top & Bake:** Sprinkle the shredded mozzarella evenly over the filling. Fold any excess pastry edges inward and brush the edges with egg wash.
- [ ] **Bake:** Bake for 35–40 minutes until the pastry is crisp and the cheesy top is bubbly and deep golden brown. Let rest 10 minutes before slicing.
