## Ingredients
- [ ] 1lb (450g) Italian sausage (remove casing)
- [ ] 2 Ibs (900g) Yukon Gold potatoes
- [ ] 1/4 cup (60ml) white wine
- [ ] 1 cup (240ml) water
- [ ] 3-4 garlic cloves
- [ ] Fresh rosemary
- [ ] Extra virgin olive oil
- [ ] Salt and black pepper

## Instructions
- [ ] Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Cube the potatoes and crumble the sausage into chunks.
- [ ] Add everything to a large baking tray. Season with salt, pepper, rosemary, garlic, and a generous drizzle of olive oil.
- [ ] Pour in the white wine and water.
- [ ] Bake uncovered for 45-60 minutes, stirring halfway through, until the potatoes are crispy.
