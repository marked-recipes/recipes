## Ingredients
### For the Pancakes
- [ ] 1 cup milk
- [ ] ¾ cup all-purpose flour
- [ ] 1 whole egg, plus 1 yolk
- [ ] Butter
- [ ] Pinch of salt
### For the Béchamel
- [ ] 1 tablespoon butter
- [ ] 1 tablespoon flour
- [ ] ½ cup hot milk
### For the Filling
- [ ] About ¼ cup béchamei
- [ ] ½ cup mozzarella, grated 
- [ ] ½ cup provolone, grated 
- [ ] ½ cup gruyere, grated
- [ ] ½ cup bel paese; grated
- [ ] ½ cup ricotta
- [ ] ¾ cup prosciutto, mortadella or ham, chopped in tiny pieces 
- [ ] Sạlt & butter to taste
- [ ] 4 tablespoons melted butter

## Instructions

- [ ] Gradually add flour to milk, whisking completely until blended. Add eggs and salt and mix well. Refrigerate at least 30 minutes, however mixture wil hold overnight.
- [ ] Melt ½ teaspoon butter in a crepe pan and turn pan to coat evenly. Add 2 tablespoons of batter to center of pan, lift from heat and swirl pan coating the whole bottom with the mixture. Cook until set and lightly browned on one side. Tum by flipping in the air or prodding loose with a fork & cook on other side one minute. Repeat until all of the batter has been used
- [ ] To make the bachamel, melt the butter in a heavy pan and add flour. Cook 2 minutes without browning, remove from heat 30 seconds. Add hot milk all at once and whisk to remove any lumps. Return to medium heat and continue whisking 30 seconds more.
- [ ] Mix about ¼ cup béchamel with the cheeses and ham together to form a thick spread. Season with salt and pepper. Place a heaping tablespoon of the spread in the center of the pancakes and roll up into a triangle. Roll in melted butter and bake at 400 degrees for 20 minutes, until very brown. If not browned, run under broiler for 2 minutes.
