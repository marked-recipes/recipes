## Ingredients

### For the Homemade Crust

- [ ] 2½ cups all-purpose flour
- [ ] ½ cup cold unsalted butter, cubed
- [ ] 2 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] 1 large egg
- [ ] 1 tsp kosher salt
- [ ] 4 tbsp ice-cold water

### For the Ricotta & Meat Filling

- [ ] 15 oz ricotta cheese
- [ ] 8 oz shredded mozzarella
- [ ] 2 oz grated Parmigiano
- [ ] 4 large eggs, beaten
- [ ] 6 oz ham, diced
- [ ] 2 oz salami, chopped
- [ ] 2 oz prosciutto, chopped
- [ ] ½ tsp kosher salt
- [ ] ¼ tsp black pepper

## Instructions

### Make the Crust

- [ ] If your ricotta looks watery, let it drain briefly before using it so the filling doesn’t become too loose.
- [ ] Add the flour, salt, cold butter, olive oil, and egg to a food processor and pulse until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs. With the processor running, slowly add the ice water and stop as soon as the dough begins to come together. 
- [ ] Turn it onto plastic wrap, press it into a disk, wrap tightly, and refrigerate for at least 1 hour or overnight. Chilling the dough firms the butter and makes the crust much easier to roll without tearing.

### Make the Filling

- [ ] Place the ricotta in a large bowl, then add the beaten eggs, mozzarella, Parmigiano, ham, salami, prosciutto, salt, and black pepper. 
- [ ] Mix everything together until the meats and cheeses are evenly distributed through the ricotta. You can also replace part of the meat with mortadella, capicola, or cooked Italian sausage if that’s what your family traditionally uses.

### Assemble the Pizza Rustica

- [ ] Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C) and grease a 9-inch springform pan. 
- [ ] Remove the chilled dough from the refrigerator and let it sit for about 5 minutes, then divide it into roughly two-thirds for the bottom and sides and one-third for the top. 
- [ ] Roll the larger piece into a circle on a lightly floured surface, transfer it carefully into the pan, and press it gently across the bottom and up the sides without stretching it. 
- [ ] If the dough tears, simply patch it with a small extra piece. Spoon in the ricotta filling and spread it evenly, pressing it down gently so there are no large air pockets.

### Add the Top Crust

- [ ] Roll the remaining dough into a circle and lay it over the filling. Trim away any major excess, then fold or pinch the bottom crust and top crust together around the edge to seal the pie. 
- [ ] Beat 1 additional egg and brush it generously over the surface for a glossy golden finish, then cut a small X in the center so steam can escape while the pie bakes.

### Bake

- [ ] Place the pie on the center rack and immediately reduce the oven temperature from 425°F to 350°F (175°C). Bake for about 75 minutes, or until the pastry is deeply golden and the center feels set. 
- [ ] Let the pie cool in the pan for about 5 minutes, remove the springform collar, then allow it to rest for another 10 minutes before slicing. 
- [ ] That resting time helps the ricotta filling firm up so you get cleaner slices instead of the filling spilling out.

## Notes
* Pizza Rustica is wonderful warm, but I also love it at room temperature, which makes it perfect for an Easter table or buffet. Rich ricotta, salty cured meats, melted cheese, and buttery homemade pastry this is definitely not a light little snack, and that’s exactly why it belongs at the holiday table.
