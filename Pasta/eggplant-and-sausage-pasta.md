## Ingredients
- [ ] 2 whole eggplants roasted
- [ ] 1 whole garlic clove roasted
- [ ] 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- [ ] 250g Italian sausage
- [ ] 1 pint cherry tomatoes washed and quartered
- [ ] 500g penne pasta
- [ ] 1/2 cup chopped Italian parsley Ricotta salata to taste
- [ ] Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions
- [ ] Poke wholes in your eggplant with a fork, baste with olive oil, wrap in foil and cook at 450 degrees Fahrenheit for approx 40 mins.  Set the eggplant aside to cook and then remove the skin
- [ ] Cut the top off of a garlic bulb, drizzle olive oil and sprinkle salt.  Wrap it in foil and bake it with the eggplant.  Once cooked, you can squeeze out the roasted garlic
- [ ] Purée the eggplant, garlic and olive oil to make a cream and set aside.
- [ ] In a pan, cook your favorite Italian sausage in little pieces.  Add the cherry tomatoes and slow it all to cook slowly together.
- [ ] Add your cooked pasta to the sausage and tomato
- [ ] Add the eggplant cream
- [ ] Adjust your seasoning 
- [ ] Grate some fresh ricotta salata into the dish
