---
title: Spaghetti and Spinach with Sun-Dried Tomato Cream Sauce
prep_time: 10 minutes
cook_time: 20 minutes
Servings: 4
---

## Ingredients
### Pasta
- [ ] 12 oz (340 g) spaghetti
- [ ] 1 tbsp salt, for the pasta water
- [ ] 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- [ ] 4 garlic cloves, minced
- [ ] ½ tsp Italian seasoning
- [ ] ¼ tsp red pepper flakes, optional
### For the Sun-Dried Tomato Cream Sauce
- [ ] ½ cup sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed and drained, thinly sliced
- [ ] 1 tbsp oil from the sun-dried tomato jar
- [ ] 1 cup heavy cream
- [ ] ½ cup chicken or vegetable broth
- [ ] ¾ cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
- [ ] ½ tsp garlic powder
- [ ] ½ tsp dried oregano
- [ ] ¼ tsp black pepper
- [ ] ½ tsp salt, or to taste
- [ ] 1 tsp Dijon mustard, optional
- [ ] 1 tsp lemon juice
### For the Spinach
- [ ] 4 cups fresh baby spinach
- [ ] ¼ cup fresh basil, chopped
- [ ] 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
### Optional Garnishes
- [ ] Extra Parmesan
- [ ] Fresh basil
- [ ] Cracked black pepper
- [ ] Red pepper flakes
- [ ] Lemon zest

## Instructions

- [ ] Cook the spaghetti: Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the spaghetti according to the package directions until al dente. Before draining, reserve 1 cup of pasta water.
- [ ] Sauté the aromatics: Heat the sun-dried tomato oil and olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the garlic, Italian seasoning, and red pepper flakes. Cook for about 30–60 seconds until fragrant.
- [ ] Add the tomatoes: Stir in the sliced sun-dried tomatoes and cook for 1–2 minutes to release their flavor.
- [ ] Make the cream sauce: Pour in the heavy cream and broth. Stir in the garlic powder, oregano, black pepper, salt, and Dijon mustard if using. Simmer gently for 3–4 minutes, stirring occasionally.
- [ ] Add the Parmesan: Reduce the heat to low and gradually stir in the Parmesan until melted and smooth. Add the lemon juice and stir well.
- [ ] Wilt the spinach: Add the fresh spinach to the skillet in handfuls. Cook for 1–2 minutes, stirring until wilted.
- [ ] Combine with pasta: Add the drained spaghetti directly to the sauce and toss until evenly coated. Add reserved pasta water a little at a time until the sauce reaches a silky, glossy consistency.
- [ ] Finish: Stir in fresh basil and parsley. Taste and adjust salt, pepper, or lemon juice as needed.
- [ ] Serve: Divide among four bowls and top with extra Parmesan, fresh basil, cracked black pepper, and red pepper flakes if desired.

### Notes

- [ ] Use the jar oil: The flavorful oil from the sun-dried tomatoes adds an incredible depth to the sauce.
- [ ] Don't overheat the cream: Keep the sauce at a gentle simmer to prevent it from becoming grainy.
- [ ] Make it lighter: Substitute half-and-half for heavy cream, though the sauce will be slightly less rich.
- [ ] Add protein: Grilled chicken, shrimp, Italian sausage, or crispy bacon make delicious additions.
- [ ] Add vegetables: Mushrooms, roasted red peppers, asparagus, or artichokes pair beautifully with the sauce.
- [ ] Make it extra cheesy: Add ¼ cup shredded mozzarella or a little Asiago cheese.
- [ ] Make it spicy: Increase the red pepper flakes or add a pinch of cayenne.
- [ ] For a brighter finish: Add extra lemon zest just before serving.
