## Ingredients

- [ ] One 6-pound whole chicken, cut into 8
- [ ] Kosher salt
- [ ] Freshly cracked black pepper
- [ ] Olive oil
- [ ] 3 cloves garlic, sliced
- [ ] 4 ounces dry vermouth
- [ ] 2 beefsteak tomatoes, cut into eighths
- [ ] 1/2 cup pitted green olives
- [ ] Small bundle fresh oregano, tied with pieces, skin-side down
- [ ] 1 1/2 cups chicken stock
- [ ] 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- [ ] Fresh basil, torn

## Instructions

### Prepare the chicken
- [ ] Set up a grill for cooking with direct and indirect heat. If using a charcoal grill, build the pieces coals on one side only. If using a Kosher salt and freshly cracked black gas grill, heat one side only.
- [ ] Place a large cast-iron pan on Olive oil, for cooking the direct-heat side of the grill to 3 cloves garlic, sliced preheat.
- [ ] Sprinkle the chicken with salt and pepper on both sides.
- [ ] Place the chicken in the pan, followed by the chicken.
- [ ] Allow the chicken to deeply brown, 4 to 5 minutes.
- [ ] Flip and add the 2 tablespoons unsalted butter garlic.
- [ ] Briefly sauté until fragrant.
- [ ] Move the pan to the indirect-heat side of the grill and close the lid.
- [ ] Allow to cook until the chicken is cooked through and the internal temperature reads 160 degrees F, 15 to 20 minutes.
- [ ] Remove the pan from the heat and stir in the butter.
- [ ] Discard the oregano bundle.
- [ ] Top with the basil and serve.

## Notes
* If using a charcoal grill, build the pieces coals on one side only. If using a Kosher salt and freshly cracked black gas grill, heat one side only.
* Allow the chicken to cook until the internal temperature reads 160 degrees F, 15 to 20 minutes.
* Discard the oregano bundle.
* Top with the basil and serve.
