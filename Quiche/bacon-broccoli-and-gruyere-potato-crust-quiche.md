## Ingredients

- [ ] 6 medium russet or golden potatoes (amount will vary depending on size)
- [ ] Avocado oil spray
- [ ] Salt
- [ ] 6 eggs
- [ ] 1/2 cup ricotta
- [ ] 5 slices bacon, cooked and diced
- [ ] 1 cup cooked broccoli, diced
- [ ] 1 heaping cup shredded cheese (½ Gruyère, ½ sharp cheddar)
- [ ] Salt + pepper
- [ ] Additional shredded cheddar for topping
- [ ] 2 slices bacon, cooked and crumbled (for topping)
- [ ] Fresh chives (for topping)
- [ ] Flaky salt (for finishing)

## Instructions

- [ ] Peel your potatoes and cut them into smaller pieces. Add to a pot of boiling water and cook until fork tender.
- [ ] Drain the potatoes and refrigerate until completely cooled.
- [ ] Spray a springform pan with avocado oil. Press the cooled potatoes into the bottom and up the sides of the pan to form your crust. I like using the bottom of a cup sprayed with a little avocado oil to prevent sticking and help press and shape the crust evenly.
- [ ] Spray the crust with a little more avocado oil and sprinkle with salt.
- [ ] Bake at 425°F for 20–25 minutes to pre-bake the crust until lightly golden and starting to crisp.
- [ ] In a bowl, whisk together the eggs, ricotta, Gruyère, sharp cheddar, cooked diced bacon, broccoli, salt, and pepper until combined.
- [ ] Pour the egg mixture into your pre-baked potato crust and top with additional shredded cheddar.
- [ ] Bake at 425°F for 40–45 minutes, or until the quiche is fully set and no longer jiggly in the center.
- [ ] Let cool slightly before removing from the springform pan. Finish with the extra crumbled bacon, fresh chives, and flaky salt.

## Notes
- [ ] The potato crust gets golden and crispy around the edges, and the bacon, broccoli, and cheesy filling make this such a good savory breakfast. Definitely adding this one to the Thanksgiving morning menu!
