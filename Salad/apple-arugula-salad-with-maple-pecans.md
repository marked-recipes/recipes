## Ingredients
### For the Maple Pecans:
- [ ] 1 cup pecan halves (110 g)
- [ ] 2 tbsp pure maple syrup
- [ ] 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
- [ ] 1/4 tsp sea salt
- [ ] 1/4 tsp freshly cracked black pepper
### For the Apple Arugula Salad:
- [ ] 5 oz fresh arugula (140 g)
- [ ] 2 crisp apples, thinly sliced (300 g)
- [ ] 1/2 cup crumbled goat cheese or feta (75 g)
- [ ] 1/3 cup dried cranberries (45 g)
- [ ] 1/4 small red onion, thinly sliced (40 g)
- [ ] 1/2 cup pomegranate arils (80 g)
### For the Maple-Dijon Vinaigrette:
- [ ] 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- [ ] 1 1/2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- [ ] 1 tbsp pure maple syrup
- [ ] 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- [ ] 1/2 tsp fresh lemon juice
- [ ] 1/4 tsp kosher salt
- [ ] 1/4 tsp freshly cracked black
### For Garnish (optional):
- [ ] Fresh thyme leaves
- [ ] Extra maple pecans
- [ ] A few pomegranate arils
- [ ] Freshly cracked black pepper

## Instructions
- [ ] Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
- [ ] Toss the pecans with maple syrup, cinnamon, salt, and black pepper until evenly coated.
- [ ] Spread the pecans on the baking sheet and bake for 8-10 minutes, stirring once, until glossy and toasted. Let cool completely, then roughly chop.
- [ ] In a small bowl, whisk together the olive oll, apple cider vinegar, maple syrup, Dijon mustard, lemon juice, salt, and black pepper until smooth.
- [ ] Place the arugula in a large serving bowl. Add the sliced apples, goat cheese or feta, dried cranberries, red onion, and pomegranate arils.
- [ ] Drizzle the salad with the maple-Dijon vinaigrette and gently toss until everything is lightly coated.
- [ ] Scatter the maple pecans over the top just before serving so they remain crisp.
- [ ] Finish with fresh thyme and freshly cracked black pepper, if desired.
