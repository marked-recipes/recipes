## Ingredients

- [ ] 2 small boxes cranberry Jell-O
- [ ] 1 large can crushed pineapple (drained; reserve juice)
- [ ] 1 can mandarin oranges (drained; do not reserve juice)
- [ ] 1 can whole berry cranberry sauce
- [ ] 1/2 cup chopped nuts
- [ ] Water (enough added to reserved pineapple juice to make 1 cup liquid)

## Instructions

- [ ] Add enough water to the reserved pineapple juice to measure 1 cup total, then bring to a boil.
- [ ] Pour the boiling liquid over the cranberry Jell-O and stir until dissolved.
- [ ] Stir in the drained crushed pineapple, drained mandarin oranges, whole berry cranberry sauce, and chopped nuts.
- [ ] Pour into an approximately 9" x 13" container and refrigerate until set.

## Notes

* Makes approximately 15 servings.
