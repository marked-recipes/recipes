## Ingredients
- [ ] 4 slices sourdough or rustic bread
- [ ] ½ cup ricotta cheese
- [ ] ½ cup shredded mozzarella cheese
- [ ] ¼ cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
- [ ] ½ cup sun-dried tomatoes, drained and finely chopped
- [ ] 1 cup fresh baby spinach
- [ ] 2 garlic cloves, minced
- [ ] 1 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] 2 tbsp unsalted butter, softened
- [ ] ½ tsp Italian seasoning
- [ ] ¼ tsp red pepper flakes, optional
- [ ] ¼ tsp black pepper
- [ ] Salt, to taste
## Instructions
- [ ] Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the minced garlic and cook for about 30 seconds until fragrant.
- [ ] Add the spinach and cook for 1–2 minutes, stirring until wilted. Remove from the heat and let cool slightly.
- [ ] In a bowl, combine the ricotta, mozzarella, Parmesan, chopped sun-dried tomatoes, cooked spinach, Italian seasoning, black pepper, and red pepper flakes if using. Mix until well combined.
- [ ] Spread the ricotta mixture evenly over two slices of bread. Top with the remaining slices to form sandwiches.
- [ ] Generously butter the outside of each sandwich.
- [ ] Heat a skillet over medium-low heat. Place the sandwiches in the skillet and cook for 3–4 minutes per side, pressing gently with a spatula, until the bread is crisp and golden and the cheese is melted.
- [ ] If the bread is browning too quickly before the cheese melts, reduce the heat and continue cooking slowly.
- [ ] Transfer the grilled cheese sandwiches to a cutting board and let them rest for 1–2 minutes before slicing.
- [ ] Serve warm with a fresh salad, tomato soup, or roasted vegetables.
