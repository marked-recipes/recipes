## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 small cake cornbread
- [ ] Onion flakes
- [ ] 1 small pkg herbed Pepperidge Farm stuffing (Blue bag)
- [ ] 1 tsp rubbed sage
- [ ] 4 eggs, beaten slightly
- [ ] 3 cans Campbell's chicken broth (or if using Swanson, etc., only use 3 cups)
- [ ] Pam cooking spray (for greasing dish)

## Instructions

- [ ] Mix all ingredients together well.
- [ ] Pour into a baking dish sprayed with Pam.
- [ ] Bake at 350°F for 30 to 45 minutes.
