## Ingredients

### Casserole
- [ ] 3 sweet potatoes, cooked and mashed
- [ ] 1 cup sugar
- [ ] 2 eggs
- [ ] 1 tsp vanilla
- [ ] 1 stick margarine
- [ ] 1/3 cup evaporated milk
- [ ] Pam cooking spray (for greasing casserole dish)

### Topping
- [ ] 1 cup brown sugar
- [ ] 1/2 cup flour (self-rising / SR)
- [ ] 1 cup pecans
- [ ] 1/3 cup margarine, melted

## Instructions

- [ ] Mix all casserole ingredients well and pour into a casserole dish sprayed with Pam.
- [ ] Combine topping ingredients and sprinkle over the casserole.
- [ ] Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes.
