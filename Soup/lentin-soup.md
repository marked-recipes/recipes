## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 package dried lentils
- [ ] olive oil
- [ ] 1 medium onion, finly chopped
- [ ] 1 garlic clove
- [ ] 2-3 celery stalks finely chopped
- [ ] 2-3 carrots, finly chopped
- [ ] 1/2 cup crushed canned tomatoes
- [ ] 1 can of chicken broth
- [ ] salt, to taste

## Instructions

- [ ] Rinse lentils thoroughly then soak them for about two hours or overnight. Before I soak them I am also looking for little stones that might have been missed in the package.
- [ ] After soaking cover them with enough water and boil a few minutes, then simmer until lentils are tender. Set aside.
- [ ] In a saucepan cover bottom of pan with olive oil.
- [ ] The following vegetables are finely chopped and added to the olive oil.
- [ ] Add one medium onion, one garlic clove, two or three stalks celery, two or three carrots.
- [ ] Simmer for about five minutes.
- [ ] Salt to taste.1
- [ ] Add lentils, one half cup crushed canned tomatoes, one cup of water, and one can of chicken broth as lentils thicken. Bon Appetite
